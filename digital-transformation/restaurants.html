Where restaurant operations fragment

  • Menus and prices live in three places that never agree — profiles, PDFs, and the counter
  • Reservations and orders arrive in DMs, calls, and walk-ins with no single record
  • Delivery-platform data never reaches the kitchen's planning
  • Daily revenue is known; per-dish, per-hour, per-channel performance is not
  • Marketing posts are effort-in with no measurable path to covers or orders

The restaurant journey along the Digitalization Index

The index applies to a restaurant exactly as it does to any business — the same five stages, weighted 30/30/20/10/10 — but each stage has a distinctly gastronomic meaning:

  1. 30%

    Xability (→ 30%): a consistent feed of dishes, offers, and stories — planned, approved, and published on schedule, with the first structured data on what your audience responds to. Xability →

  2. 60%

    XSite (→ 60%): a fast website that answers the three questions every guest has — menu, hours, location — and captures reservations and order intent instead of leaving them in comments. XSite →

  3. 80%

    XApps (→ 80%): reservations, orders, and inventory in one operational view — the counter, the kitchen, and the office finally reading from the same numbers. XApps →

  4. 90%

    XAuto (→ 90%): confirmations, reminders, review requests, and daily summaries that send themselves; supplier reorders triggered by stock levels, not memory. XAuto →

  5. 100%

    XAI (→ 100%): agents grounded in your own service data — answering guest questions, forecasting busy hours, and flagging dishes whose demand is shifting. XAI →

An example connected workflow

  1. A weekend special is planned and approved in Xability, published across profiles

  3. Reservations from the site land in the XApps book with guest history attached

Restaurant digitalization FAQs

Does a restaurant need a website if it's active on Instagram?
Social presence rents attention; a website owns it. On the Digitalization Index, social presence takes a restaurant to 30% — a fast site with the menu, hours, and a reservation path is what turns that attention into bookings you can count, which is the 60% stage.
In what order should a restaurant digitalize?
The index order exists because each stage feeds the next: presence first (audience and data), then the website (conversion), then operational systems (orders, reservations, inventory), then automation between them, and AI last — because agents are only as useful as the data the earlier stages produce.
Is the assessment on this site AI-powered?
No — it's a transparent, rules-based assessment: five questions weighted 30/30/20/10/10. It tells you your current stage and the next highest-impact step, with the reasoning stated openly.
What is XBrain's role for a restaurant?
XBrain is HorizonX's own intelligence layer — it orchestrates the journey and improves recommendations from measured outcomes under human governance. It is not a product you install; the client-facing AI capability is XAI, deployed at the final stage.
